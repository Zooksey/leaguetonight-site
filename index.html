Questions from the group chat

Which platforms work?

ESPN leagues first. Sleeper and Yahoo are next; join the waitlist and say which one you use, because that decides the order.

Do you need my ESPN password?

No, and we will never ask for it. Public leagues connect from the league link alone. Private leagues can be made viewable in ESPN's league settings, or connected with a short walkthrough that never involves your login.

What if the show gets a fact wrong?

Every name, number, lineup slot and injury tag is checked against your league's data before the episode is produced. If a check fails, that episode does not go out. Facts are the one thing the desk is never funny about.

Is it mean?

It roasts decisions, never people. Managers appear only as their team name, injuries are never a punchline, and there is no gambling talk and no profanity. Family leagues are the first customers.

Who can see our episodes?

Whoever you send the link to. Episodes are not listed anywhere public. If your league wants to post them to Shorts, Reels or TikTok, they are portrait and ready.

Is this affiliated with the NFL or ESPN?

No. League Tonight is an independent cartoon show. It says team and player names; it never uses league or team logos.
